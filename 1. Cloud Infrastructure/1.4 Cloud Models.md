Cloud model defines the deployment of the services. 3 main models - 

**Private** 
A cloud used by a single entity. It can be hosted withing the organization's own data center or managed by a third party. 
Offer greater control, customization, privacy and security
Commonly used by - banks, government etc

**Public**
Owned and managed by a third party. Resources are shared among different customers over the internet. 
Offers Lower cost,  easy scalability and no need for physical infrastructure
Commonly used by - AWS, Azure, Google Cloud

**Hybrid**
Combination of private and public. Organizations keep sensitive data in private cloud and public cloud for other services. 
Offers flexibility, scalability and better control over sensitive information
Example a company keeps customer data on private cloud and uses public cloud for scaling up its services during high demand
