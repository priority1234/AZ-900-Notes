It is the complete cloud service model from a product perspective.
It basically means renting a fully developed application.
It is least flexible but also easiest to get up and running. Requires least amount of technical knowledge to get up and running.

Provider manages everything like Application stack, infra, platform, maintenance.

User manages data, identity and access settings. It has the least operational overhead for customers.

Common scenarios for SaaS include:

- Email and messaging.
- Productivity applications.
- Finance and expense tracking.
